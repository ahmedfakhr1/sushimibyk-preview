Every cut has a purpose.

Khaled Elhady, known as Chef K, is a former telecom engineer who took a bold leap of faith to leave the corporate world and follow his lifelong passion.

What began as sushi catering for family and friends became Sushimi By K, a premium sushi caterer known for its artisan quality and meticulous attention to detail.

Then came his first artisan sushi restaurant, where handcrafted techniques, fresh ingredients and authentic flavors come together.
